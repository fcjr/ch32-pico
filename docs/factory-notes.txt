ch32-pico Rev A: fabrication and assembly review notes
Not a purchase authorization or production release.

QUANTITY AND FABRICATION
Five supplied panels, four identical devices per panel: 20 assembled devices.
One design, 93 x 71 mm panel, Standard top-side PCBA. Use the supplied panel
Gerbers, panel BOM and panel CPL together. Do not panelize them again.
Four layers, nominal 1.0 mm stackup, green mask, white silk, ENIG 1 microinch.
JLC04101H-3313 / FR-4, exact laminate to be confirmed in the new quote, outer 1 oz and inner 0.5 oz copper.
Keep the L1-to-L2 dielectric at the specified 0.0994 mm stackup value.
All vias are 0.45 mm diameter / 0.20 mm drill. No filled/capped via-in-pad.
Please confirm the quote category against those actual via dimensions.
Use flying-probe electrical test and provide production files for review.

CASTELLATIONS AND PANEL
Each device has 40 plated half-holes, 0.60 mm drill, on its two long edges.
There are 160 castellations per supplied panel, including the internal slots.
Do not mistake them for ordinary edge-crossing holes or omit their plating.
Use CNC routing, not V-scoring, at castellated edges.
Rails are 7 mm wide, separated from devices by 3 mm. Each device has a 5 mm
corner tab at the USB end and a 5 mm centered tab at the opposite end.
Mouse bites: 0.5 mm drills, nominal 0.8 mm pitch. Actual spacing is adjusted
by KiKit to fill each tab. Three 2 mm NPTH tooling holes; three top fiducials,
1 mm copper / 2 mm mask opening, with 3.5 mm opening-to-outer-edge clearance.
The USB shell projects 0.30 mm beyond the finished device outline. Review
its tolerance against the adjacent tab fillet and confirm panel handling.
No fixture or tab may obstruct the USB shell. Please quote any required pallet.
Leave rails attached for shipping; protect components during depaneling and
trim tab remnants flush. No tabs or burrs may remain on castellated sides.

REVISION IDENTIFICATION
Rev A is marked on the back; header pin 37 is labeled EN on the back.
It connects to regulator U4 pin 2. Header pin 35 is labeled PC7*; its SJ1
jumper must remain OPEN. SJ1 has no paste apertures and is excluded from
BOM/CPL; do not bridge it during assembly. Use the Rev A files together.

ASSEMBLY
26 fitted parts per device, 104 per panel, 520 across the batch.
19 BOM rows use 18 distinct LCSC part numbers. Header and test pads are not
fitted parts. There is no underside assembly and no paste on underside pads.
Do not substitute components without an exact-part/package review.
Check every model against assembly-orientation.svg and its JSON coordinates.
CPL rotations apply reviewed JLC model-frame deltas: U1 and D2 are rotated
-90 deg from their KiCad angles, J2 is shifted 1.345 mm along its axis to
JLC's model origin, and all angles are normalized to 0-360. These are
coordinate conversions against JLC's own library frames, not an approval;
confirm U1, D2, J2 and U4 in the placement preview before assembly.
D1/D4 pin 1 is cathode; LED D3 pin 2 is cathode. U1 is LQFP48, not QFN48.
D5 is TVS2200DRVR C523793, with IN pins 4-6 and exposed-pad ground.
Review a 0.125 mm stencil and the actual stencil for D5, 0402 passives and
USB-C together.
Include hidden-joint X-ray inspection. No filled/capped via-in-pad is specified.
J2 is GT-USB-7010AW, not the similar ASV model. It has SMT contacts, four plated
shell stakes and two unplated locating holes. Confirm insertion, shell soldering,
reflow limits and any separate manual-assembly or fixture charge.
Keep unpopulated header/castellated holes open during shell soldering.

MCU SOURCING
20 CH32X035C8T6 / C29779902 devices are fitted, plus actual factory attrition.
The 2026-10-19 JLC public snapshot reports 1,082 units. No stock is reserved.
Please confirm the exact-part allocation, price and lead time.
Require silicon supporting hardware I2C and ADC3/ADC7. WCH datasheet v1.7,
table 2-1 note 3, excludes these functions on specified early lots. Obtain
lot identification and supplier confirmation; do not assume any stock is new.
Retain the factory USB bootloader and reset/debug option-byte functionality.
Application firmware is not supplied for factory programming at this stage.

QUOTE AND RELEASE
Return actual charged part quantities/attrition, placement model review,
production-file review, stencil/USB process confirmation and complete charges,
including X-ray, handling, fixtures, PCB, assembly, shipping and tax.
Pin 40 is direct USB VBUS with no on-board switch or current limit.
Four 100 nF capacitors use 50 V C307331 / CL05B104KB54PNC; do not fit 16 V parts.
Prototype thermal, startup, transient and input-inrush qualification remains open.
Use external drivers with local flyback for motor/coil loads. No unknown motor
or sustained regeneration rating is assigned. See docs/preorder-review.md.
No functional production-test program
or released PD/CDC application firmware exists yet.

Diagnostic contacts TP14-TP24 are bare copper, with NO paste or parts.
Keep SJ1 open. TP23/TP24 are narrow inline USB contacts on the front.
TP15 VBUS is a 5-20 V measurement pad, not a load-current output.
Ground stitches are tented ordinary 0.45/0.20 mm vias.
RESET SW1 and BOOT SW2 use C720477, XUNPU TS-1088-AR02016 (Basic),
throughout all panel copies. Button height is 2.0 mm. The two-pad footprint
and CPL rotation are intentional. These are nonpolarized momentary switches.
C720478 (2.5 mm) is the only approved substitute; it uses the same copper.
