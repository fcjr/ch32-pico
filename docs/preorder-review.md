## Electrical and manufacturing limits

The MX26631DL, AON7264E and BSS138 reverse-blocking circuit replaces the
expensive TPS26630/LM74700 circuit. TPS3701 retains independent undervoltage
shutdown. The input/output TVS clamps, negative output clamp and 50 V power
capacitors remain. See the [power design](revision-e.md#power-path-correction)
for thresholds and [qualification plan](power-qualification.md) for required tests.

The 3 A target is unqualified. A 3 A input contract with the minimum 100 mA
board reserve allows at most 2.9 A for the external load. Unknown inductive
loads need an external driver and local suppression. The current-limit screen
is an engineering allowance, not a guaranteed limit for the selected resistor.
Native USB-PD application firmware and assembled-board testing remain open.

The four-layer JLC04101H-3313 board uses top-side assembly, ordinary tented
vias and no filled/capped via-in-pad. Castellations require Standard PCBA.
Use the generated 93 × 71 mm four-up panel; quote five panels, not twenty panels.
Review IC orientation, hidden-joint inspection, stencil, actual stackup,
USB-shell assembly, attrition and shipping with the factory. No old quote or
supplier preview approves the changed power parts or placement.
